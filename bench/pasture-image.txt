Generate a single image of a cheerful pasture scene with animals, in a clean flat-illustration
style. No photorealism. No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos or watermarks anywhere in the
image.

HARD REQUIREMENTS — the image must show ALL of them:

CANVAS / COMPOSITION
- Landscape scene filling the whole frame, nothing cropped at the edges.
- A sky gradient background (light blue at top, transitioning to lighter blue/white near the
  horizon) covering the upper ~65% of the frame.
- A grass area (green, with subtle texture or gradient) covering the lower ~35%, with a clear
  horizon line where they meet.

SUN
- A yellow circle in the upper portion of the sky.
- Surrounded by a lighter yellow halo / glow.
- At least 6 ray lines or triangles emanating outward from the sun.

CLOUDS
- At least 3 white or off-white clouds in the sky.
- Each cloud clearly built from 3+ overlapping puffs/circles — NOT a single smooth shape.

TREES
- At least 2 trees rooted firmly on the grass (NOT floating in the sky or buried in the ground).
- Each tree has:
    * A brown trunk (rectangle or tapered shape).
    * A green canopy (cluster of overlapping rounded shapes) that looks tree-like — not a green
      blob on a brown stick.

ANIMALS — exactly 4 distinct species, all visible at once. Each animal must be RECOGNIZABLE as its
species WITHOUT a text label — a person glancing at the scene should say "that's a sheep, that's a
cow."

Required species and their non-negotiable identifying features:

  COW
    - Body: white/cream with at least 3 black patches/spots.
    - Exactly 4 legs visible (this is critical — count them).
    - 2 horns or 2 small ears on the head.
    - 2 eyes.
    - A pink udder OR a pink nose (cow-defining feature).

  SHEEP
    - Body: fluffy white wool drawn as many overlapping puffs — NOT a single smooth oval.
    - Exactly 4 dark legs visible.
    - A dark face (gray or black).
    - 2 eyes.
    - 2 ears.

  PIG
    - Body: pink, mostly oval/round.
    - Exactly 4 legs visible.
    - Pink snout (a small oval at the front of the face) with 2 nostrils.
    - 2 small triangular ears.
    - 2 eyes.
    - A small curly tail.

  HORSE
    - Body: brown, longer than tall.
    - Exactly 4 legs visible (longer than the cow/sheep/pig legs).
    - Long neck.
    - Visible mane (darker shape along the neck).
    - 2 ears.
    - 2 eyes.
    - A tail at the rear.

GENERAL ANIMAL RULES (apply to all 4):
- All 4 animals on the grass, grounded by their shadows — not floating in the sky.
- Animals roughly proportional to each other (a horse is not the same size as a pig; the pig is
  the smallest).
- No animals overlapping each other; spaced across the field.
- Each animal has a clearly visible head/face turned toward the viewer or in profile.
- Complete bodies — no animal cut off by the frame edge or hidden behind scenery.

MOTION (conveyed in the still image — how is up to you)
- The animals are bouncing: caught mid-hop, each at a different phase (not all in sync).
- The clouds are drifting across the sky.

STYLE / QUALITY
- Clean flat-illustration / vector-art style: crisp shapes, flat colors, simple shading.
- Bright daylight, friendly storybook mood.
- High-resolution HD render: sharp clean edges, no blur, no artifacts, no distorted anatomy,
  no extra limbs, masterpiece-quality illustration, 4K detail.
