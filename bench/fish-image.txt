Generate a single image of an underwater scene, in a clean flat-illustration style. No
photorealism. No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

HARD REQUIREMENTS — the image must show ALL of them:

CANVAS / COMPOSITION
- Scene filling the whole frame, nothing important cropped at the edges.
- Water gradient background: lighter blue near the top (the surface), darker blue near the bottom
  (the depths).
- A sandy or rocky seafloor along the bottom (~10–15% of the frame), in tan/beige/brown.

FISH — exactly 2 fish, both fully visible, visibly different from each other. Each fish must be
RECOGNIZABLE as a fish without any text label.

Required features for EACH fish:
- A body (oval, teardrop, or simple fish shape).
- A triangular tail at the rear.
- At least 1 fin (top dorsal fin OR side fin OR both).
- Exactly 1 visible eye (with a pupil).
- A mouth indication (small line, curve, or open shape).

The two fish must be visually distinct:
- Different colors.
- Different sizes (one clearly larger than the other).
- Different overall body shape (e.g., one round/short, one long/slender).

BUBBLES
- Streams of round bubbles rising toward the surface.
- Bubbles vary in size.

SEAWEED / PLANTS
- At least 2 green seaweed plants rooted on the seafloor.
- Each seaweed is a vertical wavy frond or a stack of overlapping leaf shapes suggesting a plant.

MOTION (conveyed in the still image — how is up to you)
- Both fish are swimming horizontally, each heading at its own pace.
- The bubbles are rising.
- The seaweed sways gently.

STYLE / QUALITY
- Clean flat-illustration / vector-art style: crisp shapes, flat colors, simple shading.
- Soft light filtering down from the surface, calm friendly mood.
- High-resolution HD render: sharp clean edges, no blur, no artifacts, no distorted anatomy,
  masterpiece-quality illustration, 4K detail.
